# POC backlog batch 6: optional first-session journey

Issue #9 was re-read and its author verified as repository owner thachp. It is the remaining open backlog issue at this batch's start. Official GDD v0.7 section 19.1 and national rules were read again. User constraints retain priority: concise optional steps, no forced cinematic or audio, no new nation picker, and no credentials or live state changes during acceptance.

- Arrival offers an explicitly started globe tour with one source-grounded introduction per nation, about two minutes automatically, or manual Back/Next. Pause/Stop/Skip remain available. Reduced motion defaults to manual and changes cancel the automatic timer. Camera focus is separate from nation selection; selecting a nation or using the camera ends the tour. Navigation/account handoff cancels it, and refresh starts safely without an automatic tour.
- The government disclosure accurately describes fixed-rule AI/SI roles and current unfinished systems. Optional power/money/faith interests suggest three nations without deciding citizenship or using fictitious live population weights. Equal matches favor playable prototype economies; actual preference matches take precedence.
- Suggestions appear as badges and a Shortlist filter inside the existing bottom strip. Families and Money/Faith filters still operate on that strip; All nations remains available. No other nation-selection controls are added. Restart clears the previous suggestions but preserves actual nation choice and citizenship.
- All ten guest national vignettes provide two illustrative choices and a national-rule explanation. They are always labeled as isolated teaching samples with no real votes, money, resources, receipts or world changes, and do not promise unfinished systems are playable. Generic work/food/meal samples remain separate. Leaving/refreshing discards sample choices.
- The existing explicit nation review and oath remain the final commitment, with sign-in at the oath and fresh consent. No tutorial sample can enroll, change nation, submit a political action or consume real resources.

Model-driven AI, named AI populations, full character creation, tutorial quests and live population-balanced recommendations remain future game systems, not claims of this proof of concept. Protected harvests and next-day meal cooldown remain unchanged.

Full checks and deployed journey acceptance will be recorded after integration.
